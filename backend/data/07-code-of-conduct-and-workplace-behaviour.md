# Code of Conduct & Workplace Behaviour

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR)

Acme Technologies expects everyone to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect, professionalism, and fairness, in line with our core values of Integrity, Excellence, Collaboration, Innovation, and Ownership.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and listen to differing views.
- Be reliable and accountable for your commitments.
- Protect confidential company and client information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest and declare them when they arise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, violence, and any form of dishonesty are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

## Handling a difficult colleague or interpersonal conflict

Disagreements happen. Acme Technologies encourages resolving them early, directly, and respectfully. Follow these steps in order:

1. **Address it directly and privately.** Where you feel comfortable, speak with the colleague one-on-one. Focus on the specific behaviour and its impact, not on personal attacks, and try to understand their perspective.
2. **Give it a genuine chance to improve.** Many issues are resolved at this stage through better communication and clear expectations.
3. **Involve your line manager.** If the issue continues or you are not comfortable addressing it directly, raise it with your manager, who can mediate or advise. If the difficulty is with your manager, approach their manager or HR.
4. **Escalate to HR.** If the situation is unresolved, or if it involves harassment, discrimination, or bullying, contact People & Culture (HR). HR will handle the matter confidentially and, where appropriate, facilitate mediation or a formal process.
5. **Formal grievance.** For serious or persistent issues, you may raise a formal grievance through HR, which will be investigated under the company's grievance procedure.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a genuine concern in good faith is prohibited.

## Disciplinary process (summary)

Where conduct breaches this policy, the disciplinary process normally follows: informal discussion → verbal warning → written warning → final written warning → dismissal. Serious misconduct may lead to immediate action. Employees have the right to be heard and to appeal.
