# Leave Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR)

This policy applies to all full-time employees. Leave is tracked in the HR self-service portal. Employees who want to know their **personal remaining balance** should check the portal or contact HR (see the *Personal Data & Self-Service* document); this policy covers **entitlements**, not individual balances.

## Annual (vacation) leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Entitlement accrues monthly and is pro-rated for employees who join partway through the year. Annual leave should be requested at least **two weeks in advance** through the HR portal and approved by the line manager.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to **12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**.

- Absences of **one or two days** can be self-certified by notifying your manager and logging the absence in the portal.
- Absences of **three consecutive days or more** require a medical certificate from a licensed practitioner.
- Sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out if unused.

## Carrying over unused leave

Unused **annual leave** may be carried over into the following year, subject to the following rules:

- A maximum of **5 unused annual-leave days** may be carried over.
- Carried-over days **must be used by 31 March** of the new year, after which they lapse.
- Carry-over beyond 5 days is only permitted in exceptional cases (for example, business-critical deadlines that prevented leave) and requires written approval from both the line manager and HR.

Sick leave, compassionate leave, and other special leave types **cannot** be carried over.

## Other leave types

- **Parental leave:** Maternity leave of 14 weeks (fully paid) and paternity leave of 2 weeks (fully paid). See HR for details on eligibility and phased return.
- **Compassionate/bereavement leave:** Up to 5 days for the loss of an immediate family member.
- **Study/exam leave:** Available for job-relevant study, subject to manager approval.
- **Unpaid leave:** May be granted at management discretion once paid entitlements are exhausted.
