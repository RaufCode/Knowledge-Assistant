# Onboarding Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR)

Onboarding at Acme Technologies is a structured **90-day process** designed to help new employees settle in, understand the company, and become productive. It is coordinated by HR together with the hiring manager.

## Before day one (pre-boarding)

- HR sends a welcome email with the start date, first-day agenda, and required documents.
- IT provisions accounts, a laptop, and access (see the IT & VPN Setup Guide).
- The manager assigns an **onboarding buddy** — an experienced colleague who supports the new hire through the first weeks.

## Day one

- Welcome and office/tools orientation.
- Complete HR paperwork and payroll setup in the self-service portal.
- Receive equipment and set up accounts, email, and VPN.
- Introduction to the team and the onboarding buddy.

## First week

- Company induction session: mission, core values, structure, and policies.
- Department and team-specific orientation.
- Set up development environment and access to relevant tools (for technical roles).
- Manager sets initial expectations and shares the 30/60/90-day plan.

## First 30 / 60 / 90 days

- **30 days:** Complete required training, understand your role, and deliver first small tasks. First check-in with manager and HR.
- **60 days:** Take ownership of regular work; mid-probation review with the manager.
- **90 days:** End-of-probation review. HR and the manager confirm successful completion or agree next steps.

## Probation

New employees serve a **6-month probation period**, with formal reviews at 30, 60, and 90 days and a final review before confirmation. Full benefits such as salary advances become available on successful completion of probation.
