# IT & VPN Setup Guide

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** IT Support & Security

This guide explains how to set up the company VPN and where to get IT help. The VPN gives you secure, encrypted access to Acme Technologies' internal systems when you work remotely or from an untrusted network.

## Before you start

- You need your **Acme account** (email and password) with **MFA enabled**.
- VPN access is provisioned during onboarding. If you don't have access, log a ticket with the IT Service Desk.
- Acme Technologies uses **GlobalProtect** as its standard VPN client.

## Setting up the VPN (GlobalProtect)

1. **Install the client.** Open the **Company Portal / Software Center** on your Acme laptop and install **GlobalProtect**. (On personal or mobile devices, download GlobalProtect from the official app store — only if BYOD is approved for your role.)
2. **Open GlobalProtect** and, when prompted for the portal address, enter: **vpn.acmetech.example**
3. **Sign in** with your Acme email and password.
4. **Approve the MFA prompt** on your authenticator app.
5. Wait for the status to show **"Connected."** You now have secure access to internal systems.

## Everyday use

- Connect to the VPN **whenever you access internal systems from outside the office** or over public/untrusted Wi-Fi.
- You do not need the VPN for general internet browsing on the office network.
- Disconnect when you no longer need internal access.

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't connect:** check your internet, confirm the portal address (`vpn.acmetech.example`), and ensure your password hasn't expired.
- **MFA not arriving:** open your authenticator app manually to approve, or re-register MFA via the IT Service Desk.
- **Still stuck:** log a ticket with the **IT Service Desk** (see below) or call the IT support line.

## Getting IT help

- **IT Service Desk portal:** on the intranet (preferred for non-urgent issues).
- **IT support line:** +233 30 000 0001, Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM.
- **Email:** itsupport@acmetech.example

For security incidents specifically (phishing, breaches), follow the Security & Compliance Policy.
