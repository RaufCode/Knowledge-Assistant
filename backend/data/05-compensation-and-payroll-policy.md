# Compensation & Payroll Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** Finance & Accounting, with People & Culture (HR)

This policy explains how pay, raises, advances, and bonuses work in general terms. It does **not** contain individual salary figures; personal pay questions (your own salary, payslip, or tax) should go to HR or the self-service portal (see the *Personal Data & Self-Service* document).

## Pay schedule

Salaries are paid **monthly**, on the **last working day of each month**, by bank transfer. Payslips are available in the HR self-service portal on pay day.

## Receiving salary before the scheduled pay date (salary advance)

Acme Technologies does not pay salaries early on request as a matter of routine, but employees facing genuine financial need may apply for a **salary advance**:

- Eligibility: employees who have **completed probation** (6 months).
- Amount: up to **40% of net monthly salary**.
- Frequency: normally **once per quarter**.
- Process: submit the **Salary Advance Request Form** in the HR portal, with manager acknowledgement. Finance reviews and, if approved, disburses within 3 working days.
- Repayment: the advance is recovered from the **next one or two pay cycles**.

Approval is at the discretion of Finance and is not guaranteed.

## Requesting a salary raise

Salary increases happen through two routes:

**1. Annual review cycle (standard route).** Salaries are reviewed once a year during the **annual performance and pay review in January**. Increases are based on your performance rating, role, and market benchmarks. No action is needed beyond completing your performance review.

**2. Off-cycle request.** If you believe a raise is warranted outside the annual cycle (for example, after a significant increase in responsibility), follow these steps:

1. **Prepare your case** — summarise your achievements, added responsibilities, and any market data.
2. **Raise it with your line manager** in your 1:1 or a dedicated meeting.
3. If your manager supports it, they submit a **Compensation Change Request** to HR.
4. The request is reviewed by HR and the department head against budget and internal pay equity.
5. You receive a decision, normally within **four weeks**. If approved, the change takes effect the following pay cycle.

Off-cycle raises are approved selectively and depend on performance, budget, and fairness across the team.

## Bonuses

Yes, Acme Technologies operates several bonus schemes:

- **Annual performance bonus** — Paid in Q1 for the previous year. The amount depends on (a) overall **company performance**, which sets the bonus pool, and (b) your **individual performance rating**. Typical range is 5–15% of annual salary for strong performers.
- **Project/delivery bonus** — Discretionary bonuses for teams that deliver key client milestones ahead of target or with exceptional quality.
- **Referral bonus** — Paid when you refer a candidate who is hired and passes probation.
- **Spot awards** — Small recognition awards for outstanding contributions, nominated by managers.

All bonuses are discretionary, depend on company results, and are governed by the criteria communicated at the start of each performance year.
