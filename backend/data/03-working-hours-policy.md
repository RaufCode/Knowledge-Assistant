# Working Hours Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR)

## Official working hours

The standard work week at Acme Technologies is **Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, which includes a **one-hour lunch break** (typically 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM). This amounts to 40 working hours per week.

## Core hours and flexibility

To support our hybrid and remote staff, we operate **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM**, during which all employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration. Outside core hours, employees may adjust their start and end times by up to one hour with their manager's agreement, provided their full daily hours are worked and client commitments are met.

## Hybrid and remote work

Most employees follow a hybrid schedule of **three days on-site and two days remote** per week. Fully remote arrangements are available for specific roles and require manager and HR approval. Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours and to maintain a suitable working environment.

## Breaks and overtime

- Employees are entitled to their daily lunch break plus reasonable short breaks.
- Overtime is not routine. Where a project requires work beyond standard hours, it must be approved in advance by the line manager. Eligible overtime is compensated as time-off-in-lieu or paid overtime in line with the Compensation & Payroll Policy.

## Public holidays

Acme Technologies observes all official public holidays. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following working day is normally granted as a holiday.
