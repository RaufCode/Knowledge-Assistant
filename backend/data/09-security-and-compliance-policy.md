# Security & Compliance Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** IT Support & Security, with Legal & Compliance

Protecting company, client, and personal data is everyone's responsibility. This policy explains your basic obligations and, importantly, **how to report a security violation or compliance concern**.

## Your basic security responsibilities

- Use strong, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Lock your screen when away and secure your devices.
- Only access data you are authorised to use, and never share credentials.
- Use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely (see the IT & VPN Setup Guide).
- Do not install unapproved software or connect untrusted devices.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

## Reporting a security violation or compliance concern

If you notice a security incident (for example, a suspected data breach, phishing email, malware, or unauthorised access) or a compliance concern (for example, a policy or legal/ethical breach), report it **as soon as possible**. Speed matters — reporting early limits damage, and you will not be penalised for reporting in good faith, even if it turns out to be a false alarm.

**How to report:**

1. **Security incidents (urgent):** Email **security@acmetech.example** or call the **Security Hotline on +233 30 000 0002** (monitored 24/7). For an active incident, call rather than email.
2. **IT issues (e.g. a suspicious email):** Log a ticket with the IT Service Desk, or use the **"Report Phishing"** button in your email client.
3. **Compliance, ethics, or misconduct concerns:** Use the confidential **Whistleblowing / Compliance portal** (link on the intranet) or email **compliance@acmetech.example**. Reports can be made **anonymously**.
4. **If it involves your own manager or HR:** escalate to the Compliance Officer or use the anonymous whistleblowing portal.

**What to include:** what happened, when, systems or people involved, and any evidence (screenshots, email headers). Do not attempt to investigate or fix a serious incident yourself — preserve evidence and let the Security team respond.

## Non-retaliation

Acme Technologies prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Deliberately false reports made in bad faith may, however, be subject to disciplinary action.
