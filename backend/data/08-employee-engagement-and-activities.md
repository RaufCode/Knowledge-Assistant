# Employee Engagement & Activities

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR), with the Social Committee

Acme Technologies invests in employee engagement to build community, support wellbeing, and celebrate achievements. Activities are organised by HR together with the **Social Committee**, a volunteer group with representatives from each department.

## How activities are organised

- The Social Committee plans an annual calendar of events and gathers ideas through periodic surveys and a suggestions channel on Teams.
- Each event has a budget approved by HR and Finance.
- Participation is voluntary, and events are scheduled to include both on-site and remote employees where possible (for example, hybrid or virtual options).

## Regular activities

- **Monthly socials** — Informal team get-togethers, games, or shared meals.
- **Quarterly team-building** — Department or cross-department activities focused on collaboration.
- **Annual company retreat / end-of-year party** — A full-company event to celebrate the year.
- **Hackathons and innovation days** — Time for employees to explore new ideas and technologies.
- **Sports and wellness** — Fitness challenges, sports teams, and wellbeing sessions.
- **Learning meetups** — Internal talks and knowledge-sharing sessions ("Acme Tech Talks").
- **Volunteering / CSR days** — Community and charitable initiatives.

## Getting involved

Employees can join the Social Committee, propose an event through the Teams suggestions channel, or volunteer to help organise. Recognition programmes such as spot awards are also run alongside engagement activities (see the Compensation & Payroll Policy).
