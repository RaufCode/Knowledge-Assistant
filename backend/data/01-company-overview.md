# Company Overview

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR)

## About Acme Technologies Ltd.

Acme Technologies Ltd. is a software and digital services company founded in 2015, headquartered in Accra, Ghana, with a satellite office in Kumasi and a remote-first hybrid workforce. We design and build web, mobile, cloud, and data/AI solutions for clients across finance, healthcare, logistics, and the public sector.

## Company size

As of Q3 2026, Acme Technologies employs **480 people** across all departments and locations. This figure includes full-time staff, and does not include contractors or interns, who number roughly 60 additional people.

## Departments and their roles

Acme Technologies is organised into **ten (10) departments**:

1. **Software Engineering** — Designs and builds client and internal applications (web, backend, mobile). The largest department.
2. **Data & AI** — Delivers data engineering, analytics, machine learning, and AI features for clients.
3. **Cloud & DevOps** — Owns infrastructure, CI/CD, deployment, reliability, and cloud cost management.
4. **Quality Assurance (QA)** — Manual and automated testing; ensures product quality before release.
5. **Product & Design** — Product management, UX research, and UI/visual design.
6. **People & Culture (HR)** — Recruitment, onboarding, employee relations, learning & development, payroll coordination.
7. **Finance & Accounting** — Budgeting, payroll processing, invoicing, financial reporting, and compliance.
8. **Sales & Business Development** — Client acquisition, proposals, and account growth.
9. **Marketing & Communications** — Brand, content, events, and internal/external communications.
10. **IT Support & Security** — Internal IT support, device management, network, VPN, and information security.

The four **technical departments** are Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cloud & DevOps, and Quality Assurance. Their tools and tech stacks are described in the *Technical Departments & Tech Stack* document.

## Core values

Acme Technologies is guided by five core values:

- **Integrity** — We are honest, transparent, and do the right thing even when no one is watching.
- **Excellence** — We take pride in high-quality work and continuous improvement.
- **Collaboration** — We win as a team and share knowledge openly.
- **Innovation** — We experiment, learn fast, and bring new ideas to our clients.
- **Ownership** — We take responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks.
