# Personal Data & Self-Service

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Last reviewed:** September 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture (HR), with IT Support & Security

This document explains how **personal (employee-specific) information** is handled and where to get it. It also sets expectations for the Internal Knowledge Assistant.

## General policy vs. personal data

There are two kinds of questions employees ask:

- **General policy questions** — the same answer for everyone (e.g. "How many sick-leave days am I entitled to?", "What are the official working hours?", "How do bonuses work?"). These are answered by the published policies in this handbook and by the Internal Knowledge Assistant.
- **Personal data questions** — specific to one individual (e.g. "How many leave days do **I** have left?", "What is **my** salary?", "When is **my** next appraisal?", "Show **my** payslip"). These depend on private records and are **not** covered by the handbook.

## How the Internal Knowledge Assistant handles personal data

For the current version, the assistant answers **general policy questions only**. It **does not have access to any individual's personal records** — leave balances, salaries, payslips, performance ratings, or contact details of specific employees.

When asked a personal-data question, the assistant should **not guess or fabricate an answer**. Instead it should explain that it cannot access personal records and direct the employee to the correct channel below.

## Where to get your personal information

- **HR self-service portal** (on the intranet) — your leave balance, payslips, personal details, tax documents, and benefits enrolment. This is the fastest route and is available 24/7.
- **Your line manager** — appraisal timing, role and responsibility questions, and support for raise requests.
- **People & Culture (HR)** — anything the portal doesn't cover, or confidential matters. Contact details are in the *HR Contact & Directory* document (phone +233 30 000 0000, hr@acmetech.example).

## Data privacy

Personal employee data is confidential and access is restricted to the individual, authorised HR and Finance staff, and the employee's manager where relevant. Employees must not attempt to access or share others' personal data. Handling of personal data follows the company's data-protection obligations; direct any data-protection questions to the Compliance Officer.
